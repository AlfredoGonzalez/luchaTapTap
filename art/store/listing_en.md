## Full description (max 4000)

Put on your mask and step into the ring!

Lucha Tap, Tap! is a lucha libre game with a retro 16-bit style. You start as a rookie wrestler in the neighborhood arena, and every tap is a punch. Win fights, collect coins and make your way to becoming a legend of the ring.

★ TAP TO FIGHT
Every tap throws a punch. Fill the combo and finish with the KICK: tap the circles before they shrink to land it at full power.

★ UPGRADE AND HIRE
Level up your fists and hire trainers who fight for you, each with a special talent of their own.

★ BEAT THE BOSSES
At the end of every league a boss is waiting, with the clock against you. Lost? Train, come back and get your revenge.

★ SPECIAL SKILLS
Body Splash, Fury, Battle Cry, Headlock and more. Use them at the right moment to turn the fight around.

★ BELTS AND TALENTS
Change your mask to restart your career and earn belts. Spend them on permanent talents and come back stronger.

★ COLLECT MASKS
Every mask gives a different bonus. Some are won by beating bosses, others in seasonal events.

★ DAILY GOALS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
Complete goals every day, claim your daily reward and open the daily chest.

★ PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Your trainers keep fighting while you are away. When you come back, your earnings are waiting.

FEATURES
• Hand-made pixel art: wrestlers, arenas and animations
• Interactive finishing kick
• Leagues, rivals and bosses full of personality
• Skills, talents and masks
• Earnings while you are away
• Plays offline
• In Spanish and English

Free to play. Includes optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases.
